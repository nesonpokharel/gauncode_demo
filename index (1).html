GaunCode · Tech for every generation

Technology for students and elders in every village.

GaunCode brings computers, coding and the digital world to remote villages in Nepal, so young learners and their elders can explore, connect and grow together.

Join as a Volunteer Explore Programs
HOW IT WORKS

We bring the laptop to your classroom. Students learn to code, elders learn to go online, and they teach each other. Nothing else to prepare.
